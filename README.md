# Drip Well

Tap the well for sparks. Buy drips that keep running while you wait. Fold the well when it is fat enough and the next run scales harder.

Idle on phone or desktop. Progress saves in the browser, including a slice of offline drip (capped at 8 hours).

## How to play

- Tap the glowing well to squeeze sparks.
- Spend sparks on tap power and auto-drips.
- Fold at 1.0M sparks to bank a permanent multiplier.
